# v2.7: "Estimated" badge semantics (Phase 5-B of the final perspective UX pass)

Date: 2026-10-27 · Branch: `feat/v2.7-ux-networth-evidence-donuts` (cut from develop `0924a99`)
Scope: presentation only. No change to `lib/balances/**`, `lib/liquidity/**`, `lib/transactions/**`,
the snapshot writers or reconstruction, or the tier computation (`buildAsOfCompleteness`,
`computeWealthTimeMachine`'s completeness, `lensEnvelope`). This doc contains no personal data.

## 1. The questions

1. Why did "Estimated" show on Debt but not on the other perspectives?
2. Why did it go away after a refresh?
3. Debt on Preview (sandbox Space, 1M preset) shows **no** chip at all. When does it show?

## 2. Three things a chip could mean

| Axis | Question it answers | Where it lives today |
|---|---|---|
| (1) Provider freshness | How recently did the institution report this balance? | `freshnessLabel` ("Last checked N ago") on the compact hero row; `provenance.dataAsOf` / `dataAsOfBasis` in the lens. Not shown as a chip. |
| (2) Historical reconstruction | Was this date's value observed, or rebuilt from history? | `CompletenessTier`. For Net Worth/Assets it comes from the as-of **snapshot** (`isEstimated`). For Debt/Cash it comes from the **as-of lens** (`getAccountsAsOf` per-account methods → `buildAsOfCompleteness`). For Investments it comes from the investments time machine. |
| (3) Confidence in the displayed figure | Can I read the number beside the chip at face value? | Only right when the tier in (2) belongs to **the figure beside the chip**. Orthogonal caveats (FX, sync-incomplete) go in `warnings[]`. |

The chip (`TrustIndicator` compact, inside `HeroEyebrow`) is meant to be (3). The bug: on Debt and Cash
it showed a (2) tier that belonged to a different figure.

## 3. How each perspective's hero tier is sourced (before this change)

| Perspective | Hero figure | Figure's basis | Chip's envelope source | Tiers it can reach |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Net Worth / Assets (all slices) | `asOfState[metric]` | the stored snapshot on or before As-Of | `wealthEnvelope(WealthResult)`: `!found` ⇒ incomplete, `isEstimated` ⇒ derived ("Reconstructed"), else observed (silent) | observed · derived · incomplete. **Never "Estimated".** |
| Investments (standalone) | valued subtotal at As-Of | investments time machine at As-Of | `investmentsEnvelope` | all five (own labels) |
| Debt | `computeDebtKpis(accounts)` total owed | **present-day accounts, in every view** (temporal PARTIAL; the hero says "current balances" in a past As-Of) | `lensEnvelope(lens)` where `lens = asOf < today ? as-of lens : presentLens` | present: observed (silent). Past: the as-of reconstruction's worst tier: cards walked back ⇒ derived, **any installment loan held flat ⇒ estimated**, before coverage ⇒ incomplete |
| Cash (Liquidity hero + the embedded "Accessible cash" tile) | `reachableNow(accounts)` | **present-day accounts, in every view** | `lensEnvelope(ledeLens)`, the as-of lens when historical | same as Debt (cash/cards reconstructed, other holdings held ⇒ estimated) |

## 4. Root causes

### 4.1 Why "Estimated" showed on Debt but not on the others

The Debt hero's chip was fed the **as-of lens**, but the Debt headline is **present-day**
(`DebtWorkspace`: "FIGURES OF RECORD: present-day, from the accounts array (never the lens)").
In a past As-Of the as-of lens is a reconstruction. `getAccountsAsOf` walks revolving cards back
through transactions (`card-walkback` ⇒ derived) and holds installment loans at their current balance
(`held-flat` ⇒ estimated). `propagateCompleteness` takes the worst tier, so any Space with a loan
reads **Estimated**. Since v2.7 the verdict prose is not rendered, so on the Debt page the as-of lens
qualified **no visible figure** apart from the chip itself (the balance-history chart uses the
snapshot series, not the lens).

Net Worth / Assets read the **stored snapshot** for the same date. That snapshot is either observed
(silent) or `isEstimated` ("Reconstructed"). `computeWealthTimeMachine` never produces "Estimated"
(see §6). So on the same past date, Net Worth said nothing or "Reconstructed" while Debt said
"Estimated". These are two different reconstructions of two different things, and on Debt the
chip described neither the headline nor the chart.

Cash had the **same defect** (present-day "Accessible cash" with the as-of lens's chip). It was less
visible because the Cash section is mostly reached embedded under Assets, where the page hero is the
wealth (snapshot) chip.

### 4.2 Why it disappeared "after refresh"

The chip lived on the as-of lens's lifecycle, so it came and went with things that do not change the
headline:

- **As-Of returning to today.** The chip shows only when `asOf < today`. Before PR #57 the shell wrote
  the rolling As-Of (= today) into the URL, so a tab reloaded after UTC midnight hydrated *yesterday*
  as an explicit past date. That made the view historical and the chip appeared. With #57 a reload
  re-resolves the rolling preset to today, the view is present-day, and the chip goes away. Either way
  the total owed beside it did not change basis.
- **Data refresh.** A refresh bumps `presentLens.computedAt`. That re-keys the session cache of the
  as-of lens (`SPACE_DATA_REFRESHED_EVENT` also clears it). `useDebtSpaceData` resets the as-of lens to
  `null` until the refetch lands, the envelope resolves `{}`, and the chip disappears. If the refetch
  fails, the error path keeps "the last lens", but for the new key that is `null`, so the chip stays
  gone.

Neither case meant the figure became more trustworthy. The chip was **relabelled by a fetch**, which
the brief rules out.

### 4.3 Why Debt at 1M shows no chip

1M is a rolling preset: As-Of = today, so the lens is the present lens. A present-day lens carries no
`completeness` and resolves observed, which the compact chip shows as nothing. This is correct: the
headline is current balances.

## 5. What changed (presentation only)

1. **The chip reads the envelope of the figure beside it.** New pure helper
   `components/space/trust/figure-envelope.ts` → `presentFigureEnvelope()`. In a past As-Of, the Debt
   hero and the Cash hero/tile resolve the **present-day lens** through the same
   `resolvePerspectiveEnvelope`. At present day nothing changes (it returns the on-screen envelope
   itself). Effects:
   - No "Estimated" beside a present-day total in a past As-Of.
   - The chip is independent of the as-of fetch lifecycle, so refreshes and refetches cannot make it
     flicker or vanish.
   - Real caveats on the present figure (FX walked back or unconverted, sync-incomplete) still show.
   - The shell still receives the on-screen envelope (`onEnvelopeChange` unchanged), and the as-of
     lens, its tiers and its route are untouched.
   - The past-date disclosure stays where it was: "Total debt above uses current balances, the chart
     reflects <date>" under the chart, and "current balances" on the as-of line.
2. **One placement.** `LiquidityHero` and `InvestmentsHero` now use the shared fixed-height
   `HeroEyebrow` (h-6), like Net Worth / Assets / Debt. The chip sits in the same spot on every
   perspective and never changes header height.
3. **The tooltip states the basis.** The Net Worth / Assets chip's reason now names the snapshot date
   the figure comes from ("… Figure from the <date> snapshot."). The Debt/Cash as-of reasons already
   named their date. Copy change in `wealthEnvelope`'s `detail` only; the tier is unchanged.
4. Wording is unchanged and canonical (`COMPLETENESS_PRESENTATION`): Reconstructed = derived,
   Estimated = estimated, and so on. Investments keeps its domain labels.

Tests: `components/space/trust/estimated-badge-confidence.test.ts` (20 checks). Updated pins (each is
a deliberate change): `DebtWorkspace.test` (hero gets `heroEnvelope`) and `LiquidityWorkspace.test`
(hero/tile get `figureEnvelope`).

## 6. STOPPED: proposed tier-computation change (needs owner approval)

**Finding.** `computeWealthTimeMachine` (lib/wealth/wealth-time-machine.ts) derives the Net Worth /
Assets tier from the boolean `isEstimated` only:

```
!found ⇒ incomplete · isEstimated ⇒ derived · else ⇒ observed
```

The `Snapshot` DTO also carries the resolved `completenessTier` (resolved once at the read boundary
by `lib/snapshots/snapshot-completeness.core.ts`). The type's own contract says *"anything reasoning
about CONFIDENCE must use these, never the raw pair"*. Consequences:

- A reconstructed row whose resolved tier is `estimated` or `incomplete` (for example a partial
  investment valuation) shows "Reconstructed", never "Estimated" or "Incomplete".
- An observed row the live writer recorded as `incomplete` (an FX-unconverted account excluded,
  REVIEW-3 B-4) is silent on the hero. The current composition's separate `fxUnconverted` warning
  covers this only for today, not for a past date.

This is the second half of "Estimated on Debt but never on Net Worth".

**Proposed change (NOT made).** In `computeWealthTimeMachine`, carry the as-of snapshot's resolved
tier:

```
tier = !found ? "incomplete" : (asOfState.completenessTier ?? (asOfState.isEstimated ? "derived" : "observed"))
```

with labels/tones from `COMPLETENESS_PRESENTATION`. This changes trust semantics on every
Net Worth / Assets read (and anything consuming `WealthResult.completeness`: the AI and the Daily Brief
contracts), so it needs owner approval and its own tests. The chart's per-point basis (`isEstimated`
⇒ dashed) could likewise move to the resolved tier (`unreliable` styling already exists in
`TrendChart`).

## 7. Known gaps (not addressed here)

- Provider freshness (axis 1) is not a chip. The desktop hero has no "Last checked" line (compact has
  it). Freshness should stay separate from the completeness chip, never folded into it.
- In Assets → Investments, the embedded section keeps its own investments-envelope chip in its status
  row (it qualifies the section's holdings valuation), beside the page hero's snapshot chip. They
  describe different figures. This is intentional, but the two can read differently on the same date.
