## 2. F-1: root cause and the Sandbox-vs-real conclusion

**The row.** Plaid Sandbox Credit Card, "AUTOMATIC PAYMENT - THANK", −2,078.50 per month. Through the real pipeline (matrix tests `F-1 · …`):

| Stage | Value |
|---|---|
| Provider facts | Account type `debt`. Amount −2,078.50 (FM sign: money **out of** the card, so the balance owed went **up**). PFC `LOAN_PAYMENTS` / `LOAN_PAYMENTS_CREDIT_CARD_PAYMENT` (assumed: the Sandbox's documented category is "Payment", source PLAID_PFC, and the CC-1 rule maps exactly this detail to Payment). |
| Category | `mapPlaidCategory` ⇒ `Payment`. The rescue does not apply (it only promotes `Other`). |
| Classifier | `classifyFromPfc` LOAN_PAYMENTS ⇒ `debtPaymentUnlessLiabilityOutflow`. The CCPAY-2B veto fires (liability + amount < 0), giving **SPENDING / OUTFLOW, 0.7, ACCOUNT_TYPE_CONTEXT**. |
| Matching evidence | None. A SPENDING row is not a transfer candidate (prefilter), and no ±2,078.50 depository leg exists in the window. |
| Resolved nature / label | `SPENDING` / "Spending". |
| Cash Flow | `NEUTRAL/REAL_COST`: never Cash out (card tier). In "Charged on credit" (4,157.00) and in Net spending (3,774.46). |
| Category donut | "Payment" is structural, so it folds to **Other** (2,657.00 = 2,078.50 + 500 + 78.50). |
| Debt payments / payoff | Not a payment (`reducesDebt` false). `computeDebtService` counts it as a new charge, so netNewBorrowing is 4,157.00. |
| AI facts | Narrated as spending; included in spend totals through `foldEconomicRow`. |
| **Economically expected** | Not a purchase. Either the payment the label describes, with an impossible sign, or a payment reversal. In neither case is it spending. With no evidence that settles it, the owner contract says **explicitly unresolved**. |

The matrix reproduces every Preview figure for the window exactly: Cash in 500.00, Cash out 117.46, Charged on credit 4,157.00, Net spending 3,774.46, Unresolved 6,879.22. So the pipeline used here is the one Preview runs.

**Root cause.** The CCPAY-2B veto treats every liability outflow as a purchase whatever the provider says. That is right for the mislabels it was written for: a merchant Plaid tagged LOAN_PAYMENTS by brand string. It is wrong for a row whose provider detail says "credit-card payment" while the sign says "balance went up". That combination has three possible meanings, and sign plus account cannot tell them apart:
1. a **returned or reversed card payment** (a bounced payment re-raises the balance);
2. a **provider artefact** (the Sandbox row);
3. a **purchase mislabelled by brand**. Two of the five CCPAY-1 corpus rows have this exact detail, $584.54 in aggregate.

**Conclusion: the F-1 row itself is a Sandbox artefact, but the defect can affect real accounts.**
- **Sandbox artefact.** Real card payments arrive on the card as liability **inflows**. The lead's Production audit observed card legs of +amount ⇒ DEBT_PAYMENT, paired with the checking outflow, and the matrix pins this shape (case 1). No real issuer posts a recurring balance-increasing "payment".
- **Real accounts.**
  - **Returned or reversed card payments** have the F-1 shape exactly. Today they count as spending (matrix case 7: a returned 300 adds 300 to spending and to card charges).
  - **Payment-reversal handling does not exist.** A repository search for returned, reversed, chargeback, NSF or bounced handling finds nothing in `lib/ app/ components/ scripts/`, which confirms the lead's finding.
  - **Chargebacks and dispute credits are covered** only when the provider files them under a spend family, in which case they classify REFUND (case 7 pins this).

## 7. Authority-changing proposals

### 7.1 GAP-1: payment-shaped liability outflow ⇒ UNKNOWN (classifier v6). **REQUIRES OWNER APPROVAL.** Implemented in `fe404a0`.

- **Rule.** `classifyFromPfc`, case LOAN_PAYMENTS: if the row is a liability outflow **and** `pfcDetailed === 'LOAN_PAYMENTS_CREDIT_CARD_PAYMENT'`, return `UNKNOWN / OUTFLOW / 0.3 / ACCOUNT_TYPE_CONTEXT`.
- **Unchanged.** All other LOAN_PAYMENTS details and the no-PFC "Payment" category path still give the purchase answer. The CCPAY-2B invariant (never DEBT_PAYMENT on a liability outflow) holds.
- **Explainable.** Reason ACCOUNT_TYPE_CONTEXT plus confidence 0.3. The raw PFC, category and amount are preserved. Label "Unclassified"; liquidity UNRESOLVED; nature UNCLASSIFIED.
- **Deterministic, idempotent, no double count.** The rule is row-local and pure. Resync and pending → posted give the same answer (matrix resync tests). The row is refused by transfer admission (LIABILITY_CHARGE), so it can never pair or be counted as a payment.
- **No automatic rewrite.** Stored rows change only on a Plaid re-sync of a modified transaction or an owner-run `scripts/backfill-flowtype.ts` (classifierVersion < 6).
- **Brief.** `BRIEF_GENERATION_VERSION` and the prompt hash pin are unchanged (`lifecycle.test.ts` passes). A re-synced row changes its digest only as any genuine data change does.
- **Totals.** See §4.
- **Risk / trade-off.** A real purchase the provider tagged CREDIT_CARD_PAYMENT stops counting as spending until reviewed. The code's corpus note has 2 such rows, $584.54. Mitigations:
  - it surfaces as Unclassified;
  - a scoped, reversible `USER_OVERRIDE` restores it (matrix: setting "Travel" gives SPENDING again).
- **Alternative if not approved.** Keep SPENDING and rely on the user correction: setting "Transfer" removes the row from spending (pinned). The Sandbox inflation then remains a documented fixture artefact.

### 7.2 GAP-5: debt service nets cost-flow credits on liabilities. **REQUIRES OWNER APPROVAL.** Implemented in `f79cf8a`.

- **Change.** `computeDebtService` now routes liability-tier rows through `economicSideOf`, the same side decision as `DayFacts.creditCardSpending` / `creditCardRefunds`.
- **Totals.** See §4.
- **Unchanged.** Payments, proceeds, income, spending and cash.
- **Risk.** Low. It makes one consumer agree with three others. It only moves netPaydown / netNewBorrowing on windows that contain fee or interest reversals on liabilities.

### 7.3 Design only (not implemented; each changes totals and needs a cross-row authority)

**D-1. GAP-3: provider-INCOME destination leg of an owned transfer counts as income.**
- **Cause.** `resolveTransferAssessments` targets and gathers only TRANSFER / DEBT_PAYMENT / UNKNOWN / null rows. The serializer's income attribution sees only a *persisted* counterparty.
- **Effect.** Income is overstated by the transfer (matrix: +1,200), and the source leg is left UNRESOLVED.
- **Proposal.**
  - Admit positive INCOME rows on depository accounts as **destination-only** candidates in the corpus (never as targets).
  - When mutual-uniqueness pairs one with an owned outflow, mature it INTERNAL and feed `isOwnedInternalTransfer` from the read-time assessment.
- **Effect of the fix.** Income falls by each paired amount; the source leg becomes neutral.
- **Risk.** Payroll coincidentally equal to a same-window outflow. Mutual uniqueness and the ±0/±5-day stratification bound this. Production census first: INCOME rows on depository accounts with an equal and opposite owned outflow within 5 days.

**D-2. GAP-4: returned payment netting.**
- **Proposal.** A read-time *payment-reversal* pairing.
  - **Liability side:** a liability outflow with LOAN_PAYMENTS PFC (under v6, UNKNOWN), with |amount| equal to a prior DEBT_PAYMENT liability inflow on the **same** account within 10 days.
  - **Cash side:** a depository inflow equal to the counted cash leg of that payment.
- **Effect.** The counted payment and its reversal net to 0 in debt paid / payments. The cash-side credit becomes NEUTRAL instead of UNRESOLVED.
- **What it needs.** A new maturity leaf (e.g. `PAYMENT_REVERSAL`), a nature, and a debt-payment-authority exclusion.
- **Risk.** Two genuine equal payments where one is later reversed. Same-account plus amount plus window plus mutual uniqueness make this rare.
- **Note.** Without GAP-1 approved, the reversal row stays SPENDING and D-2 would also have to override the classifier. D-2 builds on 7.1.

**D-3. Explorer "Debt" pill / category filters (R1/R2).**
- **Option A.** Persist the resolved flow. The PR #58 repair already does this for the attested-transfer subset; extending its write rule to unattested `UNRESOLVED_TRANSFER` cash legs (Arm A ⇒ TRANSFER_UNRESOLVED) is already in its decision table for NO_DESTINATION_EVIDENCE without liability detail.
- **Option B.** Exclude unattested legs from the "Debt" pill through a materialised flag.
- **Effect.** Pill counts only; no money total.

**D-4. Cash-in-transit candidacy.**
- **Proposal.** Admit DEBT_PAYMENT cash legs that are **unattested** (`isDebtPaymentAttested` false) as transit candidates.
- **Effect.** It changes the adjusted-cash figure, so it needs owner approval under the adjusted-cash contract.
