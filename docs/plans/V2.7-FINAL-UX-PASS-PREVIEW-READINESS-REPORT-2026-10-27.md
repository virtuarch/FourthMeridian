## 4. Date-state architecture

- **Two URL-backed slices** (`lib/space/perspective-time-groups.ts`, `usePerspectiveShellState`):
  - **position:** Net Worth, Assets, Debt and the other tabs, using `asof` / `compareto` / `preset` (legacy links keep their meaning);
  - **flow:** Cash Flow and Spending, which share it, using `fasof` / `fcompareto` / `fpreset`, plus `fperiod` for a heatmap-picked month, quarter or year.
- **Defaults:** both slices default to 1M (`PAST_MONTH`).
- **Rolling vs pinned:** rolling dates are omitted from the URL and advance at UTC midnight per slice (#57). Explicit dates and heatmap picks stay pinned.
- **Header ⇄ heatmap:** a heatmap Month / Quarter / Year pick writes the unit into the header ("2026", "Q4 2026"). Any header action clears it. Cells are always days; reporting range and display granularity stay distinct.
- **Legacy links:** a Cash Flow link with no flow params adopts its period into the flow slice and is canonicalized in place.
- **Verified on Preview** (rendered, not tests):
  - Net Worth on "Last 12 months" with flow "Last 90 days".
  - The sidebar Cash Flow link shows "Last 90 days".
  - Back shows "Last 12 months".
- **Verified locally:** a heatmap Year pick shows "2026" on Cash Flow while Net Worth stays "1 month", and Back restores "2026".

## 5. Confidence (Estimated) badge findings

Full analysis: `V2.7-ESTIMATED-BADGE-SEMANTICS-2026-10-27.md`.

- **Why it showed only on Debt:**
  - Debt's headline is present-day, but in a past As-Of its chip showed the as-of reconstruction's tier.
  - That reconstruction holds installment loans at their current balance, so the tier read "Estimated" beside a figure that was not estimated.
  - Cash had the same defect.
- **Why it disappeared after refresh:** the chip followed the as-of fetch lifecycle, so a data refresh nulled it until the refetch landed. Since #57, a reload also returns rolling views to today, where no chip is correct.
- **Fix (presentation-isolated):**
  - Debt and Cash heroes read the present-day lens envelope (`figure-envelope.ts`).
  - Net Worth and Total assets map the as-of snapshot's resolved `completenessTier` to the chip (`wealth-chip-tier.ts`): derived ⇒ Reconstructed, estimated ⇒ Estimated, incomplete ⇒ Incomplete, falling back to `isEstimated`.
  - Sub-views keep the old rule, because the row tier is the worst across all components.
- **Brief / AI impact:** none.
  - `WealthResult` / `.completeness` and every AI and Brief input are byte-identical (pin tests and an import scan).
  - Brief `lifecycle` (88) and `digest` (43) pass, and the prompt hash is unchanged. No saved Brief regenerates.
  - Daily Brief freshness labels (PR #65 era) are unchanged.
