## 3. Transfer classification (P1)

- **Root cause:** Plaid PFC `LOAN_PAYMENTS` → `plaid-category.ts` "Payment" → `flow-classifier.ts:410-416` DEBT_PAYMENT, for depository→depository ACH pulls.
- **Fix:** the transfer authority now writes TRANSFER for legs whose transfer evidence proves an owned depository/brokerage counterparty. It keeps DEBT_PAYMENT for liability counterparties and liability PFC detail, and sets needsClassification for ambiguous or unpaired legs. User overrides win. It runs after each sync, and a re-sync cannot revert it.
- **Consumers fixed:** AI `transferTotal`, Brief recent activity (resolved flow, no LIABILITY), drawer/tool category.
- **Tests:**
