### Why the refund is not double-counted

The $500 refund appears on two **different axes**, once each:
- **Cash Flow (liquidity).** Cash arrived in Checking: +500 Cash in.
- **Spending (economic).** It reduces Travel spending: −500.

Neither axis counts it twice. The matching United charge (500, Credit Card, Oct 26)
is a card purchase: it is in Spending, and it is not in Cash out until the card is paid.

### Why Cash Flow and Spending differ by ~$3.4k

They answer different questions. Cash Flow is what left liquid accounts (117.46).
Spending is what was bought by any payment method (3,774.46).

The gap is card purchases not yet paid from cash. The window also has **no** resolved
card payment from a liquid account. The only candidate, the $25 "CREDIT CARD 3333
PAYMENT" from Saving, has no proven owned destination. It therefore stays an
unresolved transfer rather than Cash-out "Debt payments", per the #62/#63 rule that
an unconfirmed destination is never asserted. That is why the Debt payments panel
reads "No debt payments in this period".

## Findings (sandbox data, NOT contract changes — escalated, not acted on)

- **F-1 — card-side "AUTOMATIC PAYMENT - THANK" ($2,078.50 monthly) is counted as card spending.**
  - What the provider sends: the row arrives on the Credit Card with a charge sign (it increases the balance) and PFC category "Payment".
  - How it is classified: `flowType=SPENDING`, reason `ACCOUNT_TYPE_CONTEXT`, confidence 0.7. The sign beats the category.
  - Effect: it inflates fixture spending and the "Other" category by 2,078.50.
  - Real-world card payments arrive as credits, which become LIABILITY_PAYMENT and are excluded from spending. This is a Plaid Sandbox data artefact.
  - Changing the precedence would change classification authority, so it is **not changed here**. The owner may decide whether a liability-side row whose provider category is a payment should outrank its sign.
- **F-2 — "INTRST PYMNT" +4.22 on Saving is an unresolved transfer, not income.**
  - The provider category is Transfer, so it is excluded from Cash in.
  - This is conservative, and leaves it as data for the classifier backlog.
- **F-3 — "GUSTO PAY" −5,850 out of Money Market and the CD initial deposit −1,000 are unresolved.**
  - The provider's sandbox signs are outflows with no owned counterparty, so they stay flagged rather than silently classified.
  - This matches the owner rule that ambiguous transfers remain flagged.

None of these is a defect in the Cash Flow/Spending fold. The UI work proceeds on
the existing calculations unchanged.
