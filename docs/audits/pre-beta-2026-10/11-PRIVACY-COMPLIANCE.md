# 11 — Privacy and Customer Trust

Pre-beta audit of Fourth Meridian · `v2.6` · HEAD `cde8fa0e25d2c744933109b41611d1dd65a1f9a8` · 2026-10-21 · read-only. Finding IDs `PRIV-nnn`; the AI-provider data findings `SEC-AI-005` and `SEC-AI-001/002` are in `03-SECURITY.md`. Nothing in this report is a compliance claim; legal pages and runbooks were treated as claims and checked against code.

## 1. Verdict

Privacy engineering is functional with significant gaps. The strong parts are real: Plaid scope is minimal (Transactions only, 730 days, opaque `client_user_id`, no Identity product, no owner names or routing numbers stored), member-level privacy tiers (FULL vs BALANCE_ONLY) are enforced by both the page loaders and the AI assemblers, no prompts or completions are stored server-side, export is audited and rate-limited, deletion is two-phase with password re-authentication and Plaid `itemRemove`, and the legal copy is honest about what it does not know (OpenAI retention). The gaps: account deletion leaves sole-member shared Spaces and raw e-mail addresses in audit metadata behind; there is no retention schedule for identity-bearing operational tables; the AI legal page understates the payload actually sent to OpenAI and nothing at the point of use says the inputs leave the service; the beta waitlist form has no privacy notice and its rows are never purged; the sub-processor list omits the blockchain explorers and RPC providers that receive wallet addresses. All compliance questions (GLBA Safeguards, CCPA/CPRA, GDPR, Plaid policy, DPAs, OpenAI retention) are registered for professional review; none is answered here. Weighted privacy score: **5.8 / 10**.
